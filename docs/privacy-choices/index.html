Privacy choices

The app puts account, AI, and local-data controls in Settings.

Use the app without an account

Skip sign-in. Core task ranking, the For You view, and budgeting work locally. Syllabus import and cross-device sync are unavailable until you sign in.

Stop cloud sync

Open Settings and choose "Sign Out." The app clears the local sign-in session and AI consent.

Revoke AI consent

Open Settings and choose "Revoke AI Import Consent." The next syllabus import will require a new affirmative consent. Revoking consent does not delete tasks you already saved from earlier imports.

Reset local app data

Open Settings and choose "Reset App Data." Confirm the warning to reset tasks, budget entries, and progress on that device. If you are signed in, the reset state may sync to your account.

Delete your account

Open Settings, find "Danger zone," choose "Delete Account & Data," and confirm both warnings. This deletes your Supabase account and synced app content, clears the local session, and resets the app on that device.

If you used Sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm during deletion so WouldYouIQ can revoke the separate Apple authorization before deleting the account.

Ask a privacy question

Email milan.kinzy@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, financial account numbers, or private syllabus files.
