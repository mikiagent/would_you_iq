Privacy policy

Effective September 4, 2026

WouldYouIQ works without an account. Tasks, budget entries, ranking choices, and progress stay on your device unless you choose cloud sync or use AI syllabus import.

Who operates the app

WouldYouIQ is operated by Milan Kinzy. For privacy questions, email milan.kinzy@gmail.com.

Data kept on your device

The app stores your name, tasks, task details, ranking choices, budget amounts, progress, onboarding state, and AI-consent choice on your device. This information is not sent to us when you use the app without signing in or starting an AI import.

Optional account and cloud sync

If you sign in with Apple or Google, Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage. We store your account identifier, name, email address or Apple private relay address, optional profile image URL, and a synced copy of your app data. Synced app data includes tasks, free-form task details, ranking and progress state, monthly income, and budget entries. We use it only to sign you in, restore your data, and sync the app across your devices. We do not sell it or use it for advertising or tracking.

Supabase records authentication and service logs for security and reliability. These logs may include an account identifier, IP address, user agent, event type, and time. Supabase retains logs according to its service plan and policies.

Optional AI syllabus import

In Tasks > Context, choosing "Store syllabus" uploads the PDF, photo, or pasted text to private Supabase Storage. The app stores the source's filename, course association, and any extracted homework and exact due dates with your account. Native apps also keep a local copy. Uploading a source does not send it to an AI provider. You can open or delete each stored syllabus.

Syllabus import requires an account and a separate consent step. If you agree and start an import, the PDF, photo, or text you selected is sent through a Supabase Edge Function to OpenRouter and a routed Anthropic model endpoint. The content is used only to extract assignments for your review.

Each request requires OpenRouter to use a zero-data-retention endpoint. OpenRouter and the routed model provider process the selected content only to answer the request and do not use that content for model training. OpenRouter retains request metadata such as model, token counts, latency, and cost, but not the syllabus content. WouldYouIQ stores a linked daily usage count in Supabase to enforce the import limit. The count is deleted with your account.

AI output can be wrong. Extracted assignments are saved with their source for review; they are not automatically converted into tasks. You can decline AI consent and continue using every non-AI part of the app.

Photos and files

The app opens Apple's system pickers when you choose a syllabus PDF or photo. It receives only the item you select. It does not scan your photo library or upload other files. A selected item is sent to your private storage when you choose "Store syllabus." It is sent to an AI provider only when you request a scan and grant separate AI consent.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, cloud sync, database hosting, and server functions.
  • OpenRouter routes the optional AI request and stores non-content request metadata.
  • Anthropic supplies the model used for optional syllabus extraction through OpenRouter.
  • Apple or Google processes sign-in information when you choose that provider.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you reset the app, delete the app, or clear its storage. Synced app data remains while your account exists. In the app, open Settings and choose "Delete Account & Data" to delete the account and stored syllabus files, synced profile, app snapshot, legacy task and budget rows, and AI usage counts. The app also resets its local data and signs out.

Service providers may retain limited security, fraud-prevention, billing, and operational records under their policies after account deletion. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app asks you to confirm with Apple during deletion and revokes the corresponding Apple authorization before deleting the account.

Your choices

You can use the core app without an account, sign out to stop sync, revoke AI consent in Settings, reset local app data, or delete your account in the app. See the privacy choices page for exact steps.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Native authentication sessions are encrypted before storage, with the encryption key held in the iOS secure credential store. Supabase Row Level Security restricts signed-in users to their own app records. No method of storage or transmission is risk-free.

Children

WouldYouIQ is a general productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit another person's personal information through syllabus import without permission.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change affects AI consent, the app will ask for consent again before sending content.
