Support

For help with WouldYouIQ, email the developer.

Email support

Include in your message

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The WouldYouIQ version shown in Settings.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps that led to it.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, financial account numbers, or private syllabus files.

Sign-in troubleshooting

Check your connection and try again. Sign in with Apple requires the installed iPhone app and is not available in Expo Go. You can keep using core features without signing in.

Syllabus import troubleshooting

Use a PDF or clear photo no larger than 5 MB, or paste the assignment schedule as text. If no assignments are found, crop to the relevant page or paste the schedule. Review every extracted date before saving.

Delete an account

Open Settings, scroll to "Danger zone," and choose "Delete Account & Data." If the request fails, check your connection and try again before contacting support. Apple-linked accounts must confirm with Apple during deletion so the authorization can be revoked.
